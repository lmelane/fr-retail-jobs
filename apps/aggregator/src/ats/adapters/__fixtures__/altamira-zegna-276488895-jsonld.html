          ABOUT ZEGNA
          The global leader in luxury menswear, ZEGNA was founded in the Italian Alps in 1910. Part of the Ermenegildo Zegna Group, the brand remains true to the values of its visionary Founder, Ermenegildo Zegna, who believed in creating world-class fabrics in harmony with both nature and local communities - a philosophy embodied by Oasi Zegna, the home of our values. The inspiration behind the brand’s values, Oasi Zegna, continues to guide everything we do at ZEGNA - from supporting our people to fostering a culture of growth, where craftsmanship and innovation are core elements of our learning processes and long-term growth strategy. 

          YOUR MISSION IN THIS ROLE
          As Junior Merchandiser-Shoes, based in Milan, you will bring your unique strengths to the Merchandising Team, helping us develop the collection in line with the brand strategy and positioning, fulfilling customer and market requirements and meeting profitability goals while liaising with Design Studio and other functions.

          You’ll work closely with you team to drive meaningful results and shape the way we work and grow.
          More specifically you will:
          • Elaborate analysis on a daily/weekly/monthly basis on business performance
          • Support the optimization of stock levels across regions, maximizing sales opportunities and improving inventory efficiency
          • Develop a clear understanding of competitors’ strategies, brand positioning and pricing.
          • Collect and analyze strategic trends and key market information to identify and maximize future sales opportunities.
          • Support in the definition of the collection brief (prices, function of use, trends).
          • Support Markets and Sales teams throughout the buying campaign by preparing and maintaining commercial tools, assortment guidelines, product presentations, and collection documentation to facilitate decision-making and ensure alignment across regions

          WHO YOU ARE
          • You bring at least 1 year experience in Merchandising or similar role (preferably in fashion or luxury industry) 
          • You’re skilled in Excel and Power Point  
          • You have solid analytical skills
          • You have a strong product sensitivity
          • You communicate fluently in Italian and English
             
           WHAT WE OFFER
          • The position provides for a base salary between € 26,000-30,000
          • Remuneration is determined on objective criteria, regardless of gender.
            Within the base salary range, individual pay is determined by knowledge, skills, experience, tenure and relevant education or training.
          • The position is expected to be classified within the National Collective Bargaining Agreement (CCNL Tessile). The final contractual classification will be determined using objective criteria, including the candidate's qualifications, skills, competencies and relevant professional experience 

          JOINING ZEGNA
          Joining us means stepping into a world of excellence, where the uniqueness of our authenticity is rooted in the talents of our people, who together contribute to weaving a story of innovation and craftsmanship. You will become part of an ongoing journey of development, uncovering your talents while contributing to a proud legacy and a vibrant tomorrow.

We welcome applications from all individuals regardless of gender, age, nationality, ethnicity, sexual orientation, disability, or any other personal characteristic.
