​​PERMANENT CONTRACT – Demand Planner​ (M/F/X) 

 

At Sephora, beauty is about feeling seen, valued, and empowered, individually and collectively. It is connecting deeply with others, celebrating diversity and inclusivity, unlocking your potential and making a difference every day. Together, we belong to something beautiful. 

Since its inception in 1969 in Limoges, France, and as part of the LVMH Group since 1997, Sephora has been disrupting the prestige beauty retail industry. Today, Sephora continues to break with convention to drive its mission: champion a world of inspiration and inclusion where everyone can celebrate their beauty. 

 

With 56,000 employees in 35 countries, including 15,000 in Europe & Middle East], we connect customers and beauty brands within the world’s most passionate beauty community. With a curation of nearly 500 brands, and our own label, Sephora Collection, we offer the most unique and diverse range of products: fragrances, makeup, hair care, skincare... and much more. 

 

You will be joining Sephora’s Central Supply Chain department. Within a dynamic team, you will be actively involved in the S&OP (Sales & Operations Planning) process. You will be responsible for ensuring the company’s strategy and commercial vision are reflected through high-quality sales forecasting, taking into account input from all key stakeholders (Commercial, Finance, Marketing). 

 

You will excel and enjoy this position if you are ready to actively handle the following missions : 

​​Forecasting & Data Analysis 

  • ​Establish and adjust weekly sales forecasts by analyzing factors influencing sales (events, economic context, etc.). 

  • ​Ensure forecasts remain consistent with sales trends and seasonality. 

  • ​Analyze forecast accuracy and identify influencing factors in order to optimize sales forecasting models and methods. 

  • ​Validate the relevance of historical data in the system and document exceptional sales occurrences. 

  • ​Develop strategies for managing forecasts of products being phased out or overstocked. 

​Promotional Event Management 

  • ​Analyze the impact of promotions on sales and determine stock requirements in preparation for such events. 

​New Product Launches & One-Shots 

  • ​Manage new product launches and “one-shot” items within the forecasting tool. 

  • ​Analyze performance across different markets and adjust replenishment according to actual demand. 

  • ​Manage stock-outs for new product launches. 

​Reporting & Collaboration 

  • ​Participate in and lead meetings with local Supply Chain teams to share information and coordinate market activities, sales performance, and strategic events. 

  • ​Align sales forecasts with purchasing budgets defined by the Finance team, simulate demand scenarios, and challenge the data when necessary. 

​Cross-Functional Projects 

  • ​Contribute to the continuous improvement of forecasting processes and overall supply chain efficiency. 

  • ​Participate in the rollout of a new sales forecasting tool. 

​​ 

 

Do not hesitate to apply if you have… or if you are …  

  • ​​A Master’s degree (BAC+5) in Logistics, Supply Chain, or Engineering. 

  • ​At least 4 years of experience in Demand Planning (experience in retail is a plus). 

  • ​Strong analytical skills and confidence with numerical data. 

  • ​Rigorous, proactive, and eager to work in a challenging environment. 

  • ​A strong team player with a dynamic and positive attitude. 

  • ​Excellent command of Microsoft Office, especially Excel. 

  • ​Fluent in English, allowing you to interact effectively with various stakeholders across markets.​ 

 

 

Here, you will find:

  • Culture of empowerment, learning & growth, that offers you the tools, space and opportunity to learn, innovate and lead. 

  • Work that brings fulfillment. From delighting clients every day, to inspiring our industry at large, every action makes a difference.
