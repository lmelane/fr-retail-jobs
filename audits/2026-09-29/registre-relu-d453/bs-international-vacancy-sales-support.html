From planning to customer contact: you take care of it all!

Do you not stop until the customer is satisfied? Do you enjoy making other people's lives easier? Can you keep an overview and are you a star at planning and organising? Then you are the person we are looking for! Service, quality and speed are of paramount importance at B&S. We live up to this every day. For this, we need your organisational and communication skills! Do you recognise yourself in this? Then this job is made for you.

Reach your full potential as Sales Support

As a Sales Support Specialist, you will have a versatile job, combining administrative and commercial activities. This means that you coordinate the entire order process from A to Z. You advise customers, prepare quotations and make sure that the orders end up in the right place. In short, a very versatile job in which you monitor the order process! With your support, you contribute to the sale of our food service products and form the link between internal departments such as Purchasing and Logistics and our customers. The goal? To achieve the highest possible service level, with every customer, every order.

This is you

Relationship management? No problem. You are everybody's friend! You are the perfect point of contact, service-oriented, goal-oriented and proactive. You are the spider in the web, connecting all the loose ends. You perform better under pressure and deadlines. Bring on those responsibilities and challenges! You maintain an overview so that all orders always arrive at the right time and place. You know where your parcel is even better than DHL itself! In short, a real multitasker. In addition, you have a lot of enthusiasm and are a real go-getter.

You:

  • Have completed a post-secondary vocational collega (MBO 4 degree)
  • Have between 1-3 years of work experience in an administrative position
  • Are organisationally strong and accurate in administrative components
  • Have excellent communication skills in English

We offer you

  • A good salary in line with your experience, plus a yearly fixed Christmas bonus and a performance dependent bonus
  • Initially, a one-year contract with the prospect of  permanent employment 
  • 25 vacation days, with the possibility to purchase additional days
  • A travel allowance of up to 75 km on single trip travel when working at the office, or an allowance if you’re working remotely (from home)
  • A pension scheme where 2/3rd of the pension costs are paid by us
  • The possibility to take courses related to your growth ambitions and function
  • A coaching program focused on your mental health (non-binding)
  • Participation in group insurance such as disability insurance and survivor’s insurance
  • Free use of our company gym 
  • Bicycle plan where you can benefit from a substantial discount
